Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date you publish this]

This Privacy Policy explains how [Legal entity / developer name] ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Clubly mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique identifier from that provider. We do not collect or store passwords.

Profile information. Information you add to your profile, which may include: date of birth (used only to verify you meet our minimum age requirement — see Section 5), phone number, playing position, skill level, preferred foot, and a profile photo.

Content you provide. Clubs, teams, events, attendance responses, and messages you create or send within clubs/teams you belong to.

Subscription information. If you subscribe to Clubly Pro, our payment processor (Apple App Store or Google Play, via RevenueCat) shares your subscription status, purchase date, and renewal date with us. We never receive or store your full payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google.

Automatically collected information. Basic technical data needed to operate the App (e.g. device type, app version) may be logged by our backend infrastructure provider for debugging and reliability purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and profile
  • To operate core features: clubs, teams, events, attendance, and in-club chat
  • To send transactional emails you request, such as club invitations
  • To process and manage Pro subscriptions
  • To enforce our minimum age requirement and keep the App safe
  • To respond to support requests

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with the service providers necessary to run the App, and only to the extent needed for them to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage (profile photos)
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and entitlements
  • Resend — delivers transactional emails (e.g. club invitations)
  • Google / Apple — provide Sign In and, where applicable, process App Store/Play Store payments

Within the App, other members of a club or team you join can see the profile information you choose to share with them (e.g. your name, position, and — if you opt in during setup — your email and phone number). Club admins can see information about the members and guests they manage.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Clubly, our users, or the public.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile and associated personal data from our systems, other than records we're required to retain for legal or accounting purposes (e.g. subscription/billing history).

5. Children's Privacy

Clubly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Our sign-up flow requires entering a date of birth and blocks account creation for anyone under 13. If we learn that we've inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly within the App (editing your profile, adjusting contact-info sharing, or deleting your account), or by contacting us using the details below.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted network connections and encrypted-at-rest authentication credentials on-device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users

Our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to [support email].
